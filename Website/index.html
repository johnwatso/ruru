Know when a server drops out

If the owner stops renewing and no server takes over within 15 seconds, Ruru sends a macOS or Discord alert that the service is down—and when it recovers. You can also get alerts for failovers, UPS power loss, low battery, restored power, planned shutdowns, and Ruru issues. Participant activity joins searchable ownership history.

CLEAR PERMISSION, BY DESIGN

Taking turns
is serious business.

When connectivity gets messy, ownership needs to stay clear. Ruru records grants durably and asks cooperating apps to respect their permission deadlines.

Read the architecture
01 Permission has an expiry
A client that can’t reach Ruru stops acting when its local permission expires.
02 Ownership has a history
Durable grants and increasing ownership terms keep transitions traceable.
03 Restart means waiting safely
After restarting, Ruru waits out prior grants before allowing new ownership.
04 Graceful shutdown handover
A compatible client can drain work and release ownership during a planned shutdown, enabling faster standby takeover.
